# Stage 0 Decision — ProgressGym-Aware Design v1.0

**Date:** 2026-08-18  
**Decision:** proceed under the narrower prospective source-transport design; stop before scientific execution.

## Decision

ChronoPersona remains a viable first paper only if its central confirmatory claim is prospective transport:

> Estimate a shared early-versus-late behavioral component from independent naturalistic discovery sources A and B, freeze it, and test whether it predicts the corresponding effect from a predesignated sealed source C on unseen date-neutral decisions.

A/B agreement is preliminary. C is decisive. C failure rejects CSTG for the tested scope and cannot be rescued by substituting source, eras, item set, estimator, threshold, or temporal axis.

## Evidence

### Reported by primary sources

ProgressGym (`arXiv:2406.20087`) already uses historical text spanning nine centuries and multiple source classes, continued-pretrains historical models including 8B/70B families, applies timeless instruction tuning, and evaluates historical/value-oriented behavior. Its limitations identify temporal source-composition change and uncertainty about historical-value injection.

Other audited work establishes broad historical cueing, naturalistic decade adaptation, ideological generalization, persistent pretraining priors, final-window path dependence, explicit persona pretraining, persona directions, and point-in-time historical-model infrastructure.

### Observed in the repository

Stage 0 hardening, deterministic manifests, complete-continuation scoring, synthetic-package generation, run identity/resumption, source metadata adapters, and bounded lexical content-integrity tools exist. No real source has been qualified, no model has been trained, and no temporal result exists.

## Risks

- Real source roles may not be feasible or sufficiently independent.
- Ecological transport may be entirely composition-mediated.
- The evaluation may not be reliable at the selected model scale.
- Synthetic calibration may fail.
- A/B may not agree.
- A/B may agree but fail prospective C transport.
- Full-weight execution may exceed local or authorized compute.

Every one of these is a valid scientific stop condition.
