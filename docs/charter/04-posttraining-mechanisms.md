# Charter Module 04 — Post-Training, Mechanisms, Reproducibility, and Resources

## Neutral continuation and common post-training

After successful source-C transport, compare selected branches under:

- common base → era window;
- common base → era window → common neutral buffer;
- common base → common neutral buffer → era window.

Hold total exposure constant where the causal comparison requires it.

Apply one identical frozen, date-neutral instruction-tuning stage to selected early/late branches. Report both final endpoints and branch response to the common update:

`R_e,s = B(M_post,e,s) - B(M_pre,e,s)`

`Delta R_s = R_late,s - R_early,s`

Classify results as:

- **erased:** distinction disappears and does not reappear under neutral context;
- **masked:** default assistant policy hides it, but a neutral reinstatement test recovers it;
- **persistent:** distinction remains under common assistant policy;
- **transformed:** shared structure moves to different items or domains;
- **path-dependent:** early/late branches respond differently to the identical update.

Preference training is optional and requires a distinct scientific and spending decision.

## Channel attribution

Run only after positive CSTG, a strong source-specific result needing explanation, or a calibrated naturalistic null.

Compare original naturalistic documents, assistant-response formatting, archive attribution, and pinned synthetic transformation. Do not attribute an omnibus difference to “speaker identity”: role tokens, sequence position, loss masking, target-token exposure, discourse coherence, teacher phrasing, and synthetic provenance may differ.

After an omnibus effect, isolate one factor at a time.

## Mechanistic gate

Substantial mechanism work is blocked until source-C behavioral transport succeeds.

A temporal representation requires more than a classifier or correlational probe. It must:

- show same-era convergence across independently trained sources;
- exceed cross-era, cross-source, matched-style, and random-subspace nulls;
- be learned without source-C outcomes and predict C;
- predict an unseen behavioral domain;
- survive factual-era and lexical-register residualization;
- differ from a prompt-induced historical-role direction;
- causally change frozen behavior through injection, projection, or ablation;
- preserve general capability.

Layer, regularization, rank, and intervention strength are chosen on development data only.

## Statistical and reporting rules

The independently trained branch is the experimental unit. Items, paraphrases, option orders, samples, and repeated evaluations do not create independent model replications.

Use hierarchical or cluster-aware uncertainty, branch-level randomization, source heterogeneity, reliability/noise ceilings, effect intervals, equivalence tests for nulls, and complete seed/failure reporting.

Do not drop an unfavorable completed seed. Predefine engineering failure, rerun eligibility, partial-checkpoint treatment, missing outputs, multiplicity, and the smallest effect of interest.

Every material report contains Decision, Evidence, Artifacts, Validation, Risks, and exactly one next write-active deliverable.

## Reproducibility

Every run records immutable run ID, Git commit, full configuration, environment, model/tokenizer revisions, base/insertion checkpoint, data/evaluation hashes, condition ID, seed, hardware, precision, objective, optimizer, schedule, target tokens, sequence length, start/end state, logs, checkpoints, raw metrics, failures, exclusions, and report hashes.

No silent fallback to another model, revision, tokenizer, prompt, precision, device, source, item subset, or scorer. Fail loudly on missing logits, truncation, hash mismatch, incomplete data, incompatible tokenizer, missing branches, malformed output, checkpoint corruption, or label leakage.

## AI, legal, and ethical boundaries

Record material AI assistance in literature discovery, design, code, data-filter design, item drafting, statistical implementation, interpretation, and manuscript drafting, including human verification.

For each corpus record steward, access method, timestamp semantics/confidence, license, attribution, research/training/redistribution permission, authorship provenance, extraction, filtering, transformations, counts, and hashes.

Do not assume public access permits reuse, one platform has one license, crawl time equals publication time, modern text is human-authored, or derived-weight rights are obvious. Do not ingest private correspondence, credentials, private user data, restricted datasets, or sensitive personal records.

## Hardware and spending

Default authorization is C$0 and one training job at a time.

- RTX 2060 machine: control-plane, manifests, cleaning, statistics, tests, small inference.
- Borrowed RTX 5070 machine: only after access is confirmed and for measured evaluation, calibration development, local reconnaissance, or training benchmarks.
- Cloud: only frozen evidence-bearing jobs that cannot run locally.

Spending gates require explicit authorization before each cumulative tier: C$50 benchmark, C$250 calibration/A-B pilot, C$600 C/post-training, C$1,000 one defined credibility upgrade. Do not spend on extra eras, uncontrolled sweeps, giant generation sets, or mechanism fishing.

## Stop rules

One rescue is allowed for measurement/calibration/source feasibility/A-B scale or dose when predefined. No rescue by replacing C after outcomes. A post-training null lowers the persistence claim but does not erase valid pre-SFT CSTG. Mechanistic failure does not invalidate replicated behavioral transport.
