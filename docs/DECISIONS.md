# Decision Log

Durable research decisions are recorded here. Reversals append a new entry; they do not erase the old rationale.

## D-001 — Treat temporal adaptation as an intervention

**Date:** 2026-08-17  
**Status:** accepted

ChronoPersona will study behavior caused by a defined corpus intervention. It will not claim to reconstruct an authentic historical person or population.

**Reason:** This is testable and keeps claims aligned with what the training design can identify.

## D-002 — Separate knowledge, style, and disposition

**Date:** 2026-08-17  
**Status:** accepted

Temporal knowledge, temporal style, and temporal disposition are distinct outcome families.

**Reason:** Factual cutoff recognition and era-sounding prose are easier to produce than date-neutral behavioral change. Conflating them would make positive results scientifically weak.

## D-003 — Start with a two-slice pilot

**Date:** 2026-08-17  
**Status:** accepted

The first serious experiment compares 2008 and 2024 temporal corpora with unadapted and date-shuffled controls.

**Reason:** A maximally separated pair is the cheapest way to test whether the intervention and evaluation pipeline produce interpretable signal. The intended four-period study remains gated on the pilot.

## D-004 — Require unadapted and date-shuffled controls

**Date:** 2026-08-17  
**Status:** accepted

The pilot must include both controls.

**Reason:** The unadapted checkpoint distinguishes temporal effects from generic adaptation. Date shuffling tests whether content exposure or arbitrary extra training explains the pattern.

## D-005 — Freeze evaluation before trained outputs

**Date:** 2026-08-17  
**Status:** accepted

A versioned evaluation registry and primary analysis must be frozen before condition outputs are inspected.

**Reason:** The project has many plausible constructs and metrics. Without a freeze, researcher degrees of freedom could manufacture a narrative.

## D-006 — Prefer a cheap falsification sequence

**Date:** 2026-08-17  
**Status:** accepted

Literature, data, measurement, and throughput gates precede large training runs.

**Reason:** The scarce resource is credible evidence, not generated text. Early failures should be discovered before compute is spent.

## D-007 — No license selected yet

**Date:** 2026-08-17  
**Status:** accepted

The repository remains without an open-source license until code, data, and collaboration goals are clearer.

**Reason:** Adding a license is a rights decision and should not be guessed during scaffolding.
